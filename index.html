About

I'm not a software engineer. I'm a builder and an organiser — most of what I know came from opening Roblox Studio at night and figuring out why a door wouldn't respect someone's rank.

The building and the leading turned out to be the same skill. A base only works if people can find the armoury; a unit only works if people know who to report to. Both are design problems.

Right now I'm focused on running the defence ministry well: clear ranks, real training, and events people actually want to show up for.
